# benchhand flow definition (flow.yaml), provisional

Generated by tools/gen (`just contracts`). Do not edit. Source: [`contracts/flow/flow.tsp`](../../../../contracts/flow/flow.tsp).

- `$id`: `https://benchhand.github.io/benchhand/contracts/flow/flow.schema.json`
- Version: `0.1.0`

Provisional: the flow definition, flow.yaml (ADR-0014). The stage and gate graph is data, and every condition in it is a CEL expression: the edge guards, the gate requirements and the VOID cascade scope. The rule lists are files of their own (flow/rules.schema.json), and rules names them by kind. A gate of kind single-human advances its edge when the user approves it; a gate of kind provisional+full+human advances its edge at the provisional gate, and its human gate opens after the full check. The schema checks the shape of one file: every stage of the record's stage enum, once, with at least one gate and one edge. The flow loader checks what refers across the file when it loads it: it refuses a duplicate YAML key, two gates with the same id, an edge that names a gate the file doesn't declare, and a CEL expression that doesn't compile or type-check

### `GateDecisionRequest`

What the flow engine takes to record a human-gate decision, from the gate card (app) or from the user's reply in chat. status approved passes the gate. status void withdraws the gate's provisional approval because of a change the user named at the human gate, so it needs a human channel (app or chat) and cites that provisional decision; the VOID the interpreter records itself, after a failing full check or a MAJOR light-review finding, doesn't go through this request. provisional cites the provisional decision of a provisional+full+human gate. Every channel but auto names who decided and quotes their words, and a quote has at least one character that isn't whitespace: an empty answer is no answer. form is refused: form elicitation is not used (ADR-0018)
